Fair questions

Is this really free?
Yes. Communities pay locator fees when you sign a lease, out of the same budget they spend on advertising. You never get a bill from us.
How is this different from the reviews on the site?
Reviews tell you what living somewhere is like after move-in. They can't tell you this week's quote, which specials are live, or how a property tends to screen applicants. That's what the list adds.
How fast will I hear back?
Often within a few hours, Monday through Saturday. If you're up against a deadline, say so in the form and we'll move quicker.
Why do you ask about credit and background?
Because it changes which communities make sense. The honest version of your situation gets you a list that holds up at application time, not one that just looks nice.
My situation is complicated. Can you still help?
Broken lease, eviction, rough credit — that's a big part of what we do. The form has room for all of it, and nobody here is judging.
What happens after I submit?
A locator on our team reads it — a person, not an auto-responder — and starts pulling your list together. You'll hear from us by phone or email.
